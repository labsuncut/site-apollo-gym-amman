Privacy policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

This website belongs to Apollo Training Facility, Mecca Street, Al Sayegh Commercial Complex #181, Amman, Jordan. It is a simple information site: it tells you about the gym and how to reach us. It has no forms, no accounts, no cookies and no advertising or analytics trackers.

What we collect

Nothing. You can read every page without giving us any information. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to fill in.

What stays on your device

  • Your language choice (English or Arabic), so the site opens in the language you picked last time.
  • Whether the opening animation has already played in this browser tab, so it does not replay on every page.

Both are saved only in your own browser and never sent to us or to anyone else. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Services from other companies

  • Map. The "Visit" section can show a Google Maps view of our location. When it loads, your browser requests it from Google, which may record your IP address under Google's privacy policy.
  • Live floor count. The small bubble on the page shows how many members are on the gym floor. Your browser fetches that number from Apollo's own counter. The request contains no personal data beyond the IP address that every web request carries.
  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages, which keeps standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) under GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Links to WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and Google Maps. If you tap one of these, you leave our site and that service's own privacy policy applies.

When you contact us

If you call, email or message us on WhatsApp, we use what you tell us only to answer you and to arrange your visit or membership. We do not sell or share it.
