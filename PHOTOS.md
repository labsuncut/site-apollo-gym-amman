# Photos for the Apollo site

Every image on the site is listed in `assets/js/content.js` under `IMAGES` (hero, phone hero, finale)
and `SPACE` (the gallery). Each entry has the file, a reserved width/height, a focal point
(`focus`, used as CSS `object-position`) and the caption in both languages. To swap a photo:
drop the new file in `assets/img/`, change the entry, done. Nothing else references a photo.

Current photos are temporary: frames from Apollo's Instagram gym guide (captions painted out)
plus one red-lit weights photo Apollo sent. They are soft at desktop size.
