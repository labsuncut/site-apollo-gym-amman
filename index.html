Born in Amman

2018

The idea

December 2018: Yousef Al Masri, Mohammad Al Masri and Dorian Yates meet to create the ultimate gym in Amman.

2019

The hunt

The search for the perfect spot begins. The heart of Amman wins.

2021

The brand

The Apollo brand is born and ready to be commenced.

2023

The doors

1 October 2023: Apollo officially opens on Mecca Street.

2024

The craft

The first full year. Apollo sharpens the floor, the classes and the way it trains people.

2025

The rise

Yousef Al-Masri teams up with Abed Salameh, and Apollo takes off: the city starts talking about the gym on Mecca Street.

2026

07 · FAQ

Good questions

The things people ask before their first visit. Anything else, send a message.

When are you open?

Saturday to Thursday from 6:00 AM to midnight, and Friday from 11:00 AM to 7:00 PM.

Can I try before I join?

Yes. Trial classes are available. Message us on WhatsApp and we will book you in.

How do I join?

Call Apollo or message the team on WhatsApp. They will explain how membership works and invite you to come and see the place.

Is it busy right now?

Look at the bottom corner of this page: the live number shows how many members are on the floor right now.

Do you have personal trainers?

Not as a paid extra. Apollo has roaming coaches on the floor who help members for free as part of membership.

Where do I park?

Parking is inside the building, at Al Sayegh Commercial Complex #181 on Mecca Street, with a free valet on site.

What is there besides the gym floor?

A cafeteria, a lounge, showers and lockers. Come early, stay late.
